# WildBlocks cover image prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The character, palette, title and world themes come from the existing WildBlocks game, including `src/game/rendering/Models.ts` and the main-menu wordmark. These are illustrated covers, not gameplay screenshots.

The square and portrait versions use `reference/wildblocks-landscape-original.png` as their visual reference. Original generated images are retained in `reference/`. Final PNGs are exported in `covers/` at the exact pixel dimensions shown in the supplied Playgama publishing screenshot, using native image resampling.

## Landscape — 1920 × 1080

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished Playgama landscape cover for the original browser game WildBlocks. Create ONE full-bleed raster image, landscape 16:9, target output 1920 by 1080 pixels.
Primary request: an appealing professional game thumbnail that represents a cozy voxel exploration, crafting and home-building sandbox. It is illustrated promotional art, not a screen containing menus.
Scene: a bright grassy voxel meadow with cubic leafy trees, a blue river, layered earth-and-grass blocks, a small timber-and-stone home with a garden and a forest fading into the distance. Keep buildings and terrain plausible for a simple block-building game.
Subject: a friendly original block-shaped explorer, full body clearly visible and prominent. Match this actual game's character: square peach face with tiny simple dark eyes and a smile, brown blocky hair, tan short-brim explorer hat with a rust-red band, muted green tunic, rust-red scarf, brown trousers and boots, brown backpack with a green bedroll. All body parts use simple cuboids. Explorer carries a modest blocky hand tool, looking ready to explore.
Style: polished, warm low-poly voxel 3D illustration with crisp cuboid geometry, soft sunlight, subtle ambient shadow, clear silhouettes, gentle earthy material detail. No photorealism or overcomplicated textures. Forest green, meadow green, warm wood brown, sky blue and golden sunlight, matching WildBlocks' cozy visual identity.
Composition: wide vista with the large explorer in the foreground and the home, river and forest behind; clear open sky area for title. Give the scene depth. Strong visual hierarchy recognizable at small card sizes; do not clutter with props. Keep every essential element and all typography inside a 7 percent safe inset.
Text verbatim: "WildBlocks" as one large, highly legible playful rounded bold wordmark, with Wild colored fresh green and Blocks warm golden yellow, dark-brown outline and modest dimensional shadow. Beneath it in smaller cream lettering: "A Tiny Living World". Spell both exactly. The title is already the real game brand; invent no further copy.
Constraints: exactly one completed cover with no borders, no UI, no Playgama logo, no badges, no upload buttons, no fake screenshots, no extraneous words, no third-party logos or recognizable branded characters. Do not copy any known voxel game's logo or characters. Opaque background.
```
