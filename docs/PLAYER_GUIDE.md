## Start here

### 🌍 Choose your adventure

New World starts a saved survival adventure: gather, craft, explore and make a home. Choose a name, difficulty and optional seed; the same seed and generation version recreate the same terrain. Build Home creates a separate creative project with unlimited materials. PvP offers Battle or Build together. Deathmatch is a timed Red-versus-Blue gun battle in City Clash.

### 🧭 Your first day

Break tall grass for fibre and seeds, gather wood, and follow the quest hints. Open Backpack → Crafting to make Planks, Rope, a Scrap Axe and a Workbench. Build shelter, place torches and craft a Cozy Cot before night. Eat food to recover; the target prompt explains what you can do and which tool is needed.

### 🎯 Aim, use and interact

Aim at something close enough to reach. Break/attack gathers resources or hits enemies; use/place chooses the action for your held item; interact opens containers, stations and beds, harvests ripe plants, feeds creatures or boards boats. A red placement preview means the position is blocked. Check the controls below for your device.

### 🎒 Keep useful items ready

The first nine inventory slots are your hotbar. Select a slot before using an item; keep a tool, food, blocks and torches handy. Equipment has its own slots. Drops are collected when you approach them and have room. A full inventory can prevent pickup or crafting.

### 🖱️ Mouse-look and menus

Click into the game to capture the mouse. The Free cursor key releases it so you can click the HUD; press it again to resume looking. While the cursor is free, movement and world actions are blocked. Escape opens Pause. Single-player simulation pauses in overlays; multiplayer continues while you browse menus.

### 🆘 Stuck in a river, cave or build?

Open Pause and choose Quick Respawn, just below Resume or Keep building. It rescues you while alive and keeps your current Vitality, Energy, items and progress. Survival returns you to your set Home, otherwise starting camp; Build Home returns you to the lot entrance with both cameras moved. Friend rooms use their existing rescue near other players or a safe arena spot, with combat checks and an eight-second cooldown. You may need to wait for the destination terrain; a message explains when rescue cannot proceed. This button is available on desktop, phones and tablets. Deathmatch does not offer Quick Respawn.

### ⚙️ Make the game comfortable

Settings includes graphics, separate sound volumes, sensitivity, camera behavior, touch controls and optional gyro aiming. Accessibility includes UI scale, subtitles, reduced shake, reduced flashing and a stronger outline. Actions listed under Settings → Controls can be rebound; fixed build-tool and Deathmatch shortcuts use their own controls.

## Survival

### 🪓 Gather and upgrade

Hold break/attack to chop, mine or dig. Chop a tree’s lowest log to topple the tree and collect its drops. Some blocks and ores need a particular tool and tier; using the wrong one may yield no drops. Tools wear out: watch durability and progress through Scrap, Copper, Tempered and Crystal equipment.

### 🔨 Recipe Book and stations

Backpack → Crafting shows every recipe, including locked ones. Search by product or ingredient, then check ingredients, station, Homestead level and output space. Craft one, a small batch or All. Stand within four blocks of a required station. Workbenches, Forges, Cooking Fires, Stone Cutters and later stations unlock different recipes; browsing a station tab does not make that station nearby.

### 📦 Backpack and storage

Desktop: click to pick up/drop a stack, right-click to split a stack or place one, and Shift-click to transfer quickly. Touch: tap slots to pick up/drop, and press and hold to split. Chests have separate storage and Take All. Equip an Explorer Pack for eight extra backpack slots. Drop throws one item; hold the crouch key while dropping to throw the whole stack.

### 🏡 Shelter and home

Place walls, a roof, a door and light. The shelter icon shows when your location is sheltered and recovery improves. Walk within about three blocks of a Cozy Cot: a Sleep / Set home button appears on desktop, phone and tablet. Tap it or use Interact, then choose Sleep until morning or Set home only. Survival sleep works at night; nearby hostiles prevent sleep. Furniture around the bed adds Comfort and extends Well Rested. In shared worlds the host checks sleep requests and synchronizes morning for everyone. Your home marker helps you return.

### 🪑 Sit and stand

Walk close to a Chair and tap Sit, or aim at it and use Interact. Your player sits and can look around. Tap Stand up, use Interact, jump or move to leave the chair. Leave clear space beside and above it. Damage makes you try to stand. Saving while seated resumes beside the chair. Build Home chairs, stools and sofas support sitting in player view or Walkthrough; return from Build camera or Photo mode to use furniture.

### 🍲 Vitality, energy and food

Vitality is health. Energy supports work and sprinting; low energy makes these harder but does not starve you to death. Select food and use it to eat. Cook meals at the appropriate station for stronger healing and temporary buffs. Food, shelter and a good night’s rest help you recover.

### 🍖 Creature loot and meat

Defeated wild animals and flesh creatures always drop meat named for their species. Walk over the drop with room in your backpack to collect it. Raw meat restores 2 Vitality and 8 Energy; cook one raw meat at a nearby Cooking Fire to get cooked meat restoring 18 Vitality and 30 Energy. Glowbugs, shadow creatures and undead drop useful materials instead. Skeleton bones can be crafted into arrows. Feeding, petting and friendly gifts still work; attacking wildlife reduces Harmony. Your tamed Fenn keeps its protection and does not become a meat source.

### 🌱 Grow crops

Use a hoe on grass or dirt, select a suitable seed or root, then use it on farmland. Water with a Watering Can, nearby water or rain on exposed soil. Dry crops grow more slowly. Growth follows game time even when the chunk is unloaded; time does not advance while the world is closed. Interact with ripe crops to harvest and automatically replant. Berry bushes regrow after harvesting.

### 🐾 Feed and befriend

Hold a favorite food and interact with a friendly creature. Mossbacks like Sunapple, Fibre and Sunwheat; Pufftails like Bramble Berries and Emberroot; Twiglets like Grass Seeds and Sunwheat Seeds; Mudsnouts like Mushrooms, Puffgourd and Emberroot; Glowbugs like Glow Crystal. Fenns like Bramble Berries and Sunapple; repeated feeding can make one your companion. Happy creatures may follow or leave gifts.

### 🍃 Harmony and Homestead

Building, farming, friendships and discoveries raise Homestead level and unlock recipes. Planting trees and feeding animals improve Nature Harmony and encourage wildlife; excessive harvesting reduces it. The journal and quest hints show your progress. A companion can warn of danger and help you explore.

### 🌙 Night and caves

Explorer and Adventurer nights bring Zombies, Skeletons, Gloomstalkers, Duskfangs and other hostile creatures. Zombies shamble and hit harder; Skeletons run faster and strike at close range. Prepare shelter, weapons and torches before dusk. Hunting packs can arrive around midnight and shy night creatures retreat at dawn. Peaceful and peaceful-creature settings suppress hostile spawning. Roofs and light discourage spawning, but do not make you invincible. Bring torches into caves and watch drops, water and cave creatures. Existing worlds retain their original cave-generation version.

### 🛡️ Fight and protect yourself

Equip armor in head, body, legs and feet slots and a shield in the off-hand slot. A matching full armor set adds protection. Shields can block frontal hits; they do not guarantee every block. Swords sweep enemies in front of you, with weaker damage to extra targets; a falling strike can critically hit. Select a bow and use it to fire an arrow from your inventory. Some fired arrows can be recovered.

### ⛵ Rowboats

Craft a Rowboat at a Workbench using five Planks and two Rope. Place it on water and interact to board. Steer with movement keys or the joystick relative to the camera. Jump or interact to step out; hit the boat twice to pick it up. You cannot dig or build while seated. Boats are saved with the world.

### 🗺️ Map, journal and discoveries

The map reveals explored areas. Drag to pan and use the zoom buttons; desktop also supports the wheel and double-click to set a compass waypoint. Clear Waypoint removes it. The compass shows Home, quest targets, waypoints and your lost-satchel marker. The Discovery Journal records creatures, plants, materials, landmarks, lore and achievements. Interact with Whisper Steles to read their fragments.

### 💀 Difficulty and recovery

Death opens a screen that waits for you. Choose Respawn at home (if you have set one) or Respawn at starting camp when you are ready. Save & Quit keeps you defeated until you choose to respawn after reopening the world. You return with 75% Vitality, at least 60 Energy and three seconds of protection. Movement waits for the ground to load. Peaceful: death drops no items. Explorer: lose half of gathered materials, blocks, seeds and food in backpack slots; hotbar, tools and equipment stay. Adventurer: all hotbar and backpack stacks drop; equipped gear stays. Follow the lost-satchel marker to recover drops. Dropped items disappear after five minutes of active simulation. Peaceful-creature settings can also reduce danger. Deathmatch has its own five-second cooldown before its manual Respawn button becomes available.

## Build Home

### 🏡 Your creative lot

Create a named project, choose one of seven plot styles and a time of day. Your flat lot is 256 × 256 blocks with scenery around it. Materials and furniture are unlimited; there is no survival damage, hunger or crafting. Projects are saved separately from survival worlds. Build progress and blueprints belong to this browser/device.

### 📚 Catalog, variants and favorites

Open the Build Catalog, browse categories or search, choose a material or color, then select the item to place. Favorite items and use recent slots for repeat work. On touch, the first tap opens item details; tap again or choose Place. House templates and furniture presets place several parts together, so check their preview and clear enough space.

### 🧱 Place, paint, move and pick

Click or tap to place; hold to repeat. Rotate before placing, remove with the Remove tool, select objects to move them, use Paint to change a material, and Pick to reuse a material you can see. Doors and windows cut their own wall openings. Cancel or deselect before interacting with existing objects. A red ghost means placement is blocked.

### 📐 Build larger pieces

Wall, Room, Floor, Roof, Stairs and Pool tools use start/end points. Choose roof style, wall height, stair width or pool depth in tool options. Landscape brushes shape a selected area; Measure checks distances. Bracket keys adjust the current tool’s size on desktop. Use the touch tool drawer for the same tool choices and settings.

### ↶ Undo, selections and blueprints

Undo/Redo works on complete tool actions, including door/window openings, with up to 150 history steps in the current session. Select areas to copy, paste, rotate or delete them; duplicate selected furniture. Save selections as blueprints to reuse across projects. Large regions are capped and large deletions ask for confirmation. Undo history is not a saved backup and starts fresh when you reopen a project.

### 🕊️ Walk, fly and build camera

Double-tap Space on desktop or the Jump button on touch, or use Fly to enter flight. In player flight, Jump rises, Sprint descends and Ctrl moves faster (default Space/Shift). Touch has up/down buttons. Switch to the Build camera to position the view freely; desktop right-drag looks, Alt+right-drag orbits, middle-drag pans and the wheel zooms. The player-camera view key switches first/third person without switching the Build camera.

### 💡 Make the home work

Interact with doors and gates to open them, lights to switch them, and supported appliances to use them. Walk close to beds to see Sleep / Set home and advance to morning, even with daylight locked; chairs, stools and sofas show Sit. Use these in player view or Walkthrough, and tap Stand up, jump, move or Interact to leave a seat. Door clicks and ripe-crop clicks also work directly when the appropriate mode is active. Photo mode offers presentation controls and a time slider. Turn these modes off to resume normal building.

### 🌾 Creative farming

The Farming category includes crops, soil, hay, coops, decorations and a Farmstead template. Farm Plot creates planted fields with irrigation and fences. Choose Growing crops for live growth and harvest when ripe; fixed growth stages are decorative. Creative harvests replant and track your activity without a survival inventory.

### 📍 Coordinates and friends

Open the project to friends to build together. The top-left radar tracks friends relative to your player: you are the white arrow, friends are coloured dots, distant friends appear as rim arrows with distance, and ▲/▼ shows a different height. The label names the nearest friend; no one nearby means no recent friend position is available. The radar hides in Photo mode or when the HUD is hidden. Settings → location controls can move you to coordinates on the lot or to a friend in shared building. Enter X/Z to use the ground surface, or a valid height for an elevated destination. The host’s original project keeps saving; guests do not get a local project copy. Keep the host connected.

## PvP & shared worlds

### 🎮 Create or join a room

Choose PvP Mode → Battle or Build together to create a room. To share your own map or Build Home project, open Settings → Play with Friends → Open to friends. Read the six-character room code in the in-game room bar and send it to your friend. They choose Main menu → Join Friend’s World, enter their name and the code, then Join game; no invite link is needed. The Worlds screen also has a Join Friend’s World with Code button. The host’s map and game style are selected automatically. Use Online on both devices and keep the host connected. This device connects tabs of the same browser/site for testing. Connection failures can depend on the network or signaling service.

### ⚔️ Battle rules

The host chooses Wild World or a flat arena, equipment kit, takedown limit, creatures and whether building is allowed. Shared maps, Build together and Battle have no time limit; only Deathmatch is timed. Battle can end at the selected takedown limit; choose No takedown limit for an ongoing room. Defeated players stay on the death screen until they choose Respawn while the round is active, with full Vitality, full Energy, the chosen kit and three seconds of protection. Movement waits for the ground to load. Battle gear drops where you fell and anyone can collect it; Build together does not drop gear. A death that ends a Battle shows the results instead. Check the scoreboard for progress and the radar for players. Quick Respawn is available for getting stuck, subject to its cooldown and match rules.

### 🤝 Build together and saved worlds

Build together turns off player-versus-player damage; enabled creatures can still be dangerous in survival-style worlds. You can also open a saved survival world to friends from Settings → Play with Friends: this makes a multiplayer copy and does not write changes back to the original survival save. Opening a Build Home project keeps saving to the host’s project.

### ⏱️ Stay connected

Multiplayer does not pause when a player opens Backpack, Pause or Settings. Keep your tab in the foreground; mobile browsers may suspend it when you switch apps or lock the screen. The host leaving ends the session. Match worlds are temporary; shared Build Home is the saved-project exception. Casual peer-hosted matches rely on players playing fairly.

### 🎤 Voice chat

Listening and speaking are separate. Use the Listen button to hear others and Talk to speak; enable your microphone when asked. Push-to-talk speaks while held; Open mic stays active until turned off. Options offer player mute and supported voice-range controls. Microphone access needs browser permission and a secure site (HTTPS or localhost). Voice is optional.

## Deathmatch

### 🏙️ Lobby and City Clash

Choose Deathmatch to create/join a Deathmatch room by code or link; its codes are separate from shared-world rooms. Pick Red or Blue with Switch or an empty team slot. Up to eight players can join, with four per team; the host needs at least one player on each team to start. Keep the game open while everyone loads: slower devices have up to 90 seconds before the host removes a player who has not finished. The countdown starts as soon as everyone is ready. After ten seconds you spawn in City Clash; more distant terrain continues streaming. This arena has no block editing, survival backpack, crafting or world saves.

### 🔫 Weapons and aiming

Start with a sword and collect guns around the city. M416, AK-47, MP5, Pump Shotgun and M24 have different range and fire behavior. Aim for more control; the M24 has a 4× scope. Reload when needed, and watch ammunition. Gun pickup is manual using the pickup action; nearby ammo and medkits collect automatically when you have room.

### 🩹 Healing and respawning

Small medkits restore 30 health in 0.6 seconds; large kits restore 65 in two seconds. Movement, firing or taking damage interrupts healing. You can carry three small and two large kits. Health stations restore 20 health and have a cooldown. Death opens a screen with a five-second cooldown. When it ends, choose Respawn whenever you are ready to return with brief spawn protection and a randomized gun loadout. Waiting or reconnecting does not respawn you automatically. There is no Quick Respawn or teleport in Deathmatch.

### 🏆 Scores and results

The host chooses a 5, 8, 10 or 12-minute match. Most team eliminations at the end wins; a tied team score is a draw. MVP uses kills, then fewer deaths, with shared MVP for a remaining tie. Open the scoreboard to see teams and scores. Deathmatch keeps running while your options are open; leaving the host ends the room.

### 🎧 Team voice

Deathmatch voice options let you choose team/all audiences for listening and talking, use push-to-talk or Open mic, and mute players. Listening does not require you to speak. Check the audience before talking, grant microphone permission only if you want voice, and keep the session in the foreground.

## Devices & smooth play

### 💻 Desktop and laptops

Use a current browser with WebGL 2 and keyboard/mouse. Click the game for mouse-look and use Free cursor for HUD buttons. Settings → Controls changes supported key bindings and sensitivity. Low-powered laptops can start with Low graphics and Auto quality. Gamepad controls are not implemented.

### 📱 Phones and tablets

Play in landscape. The left joystick moves; push toward its rim to sprint. Drag the right side to look. A quick world tap uses/places/interacts or attacks; hold to break. Dedicated use, interact, jump and crouch buttons are also available. Tap hotbar slots and use menu buttons for Backpack, Map and Journal. Available fullscreen/orientation behavior depends on the browser.

### 🧱 Touch building and combat

Build Home: use the catalog, tool drawer, place, rotate, undo and interact buttons; double-tap Jump or choose Fly, then use up/down. Deathmatch: hold Fire and slide to aim, use Aim/Scope, Reload, weapon switch, pickup, medkit and scoreboard buttons. The displayed controls change by mode; survival and Deathmatch pickup actions are different.

### 📐 Optional gyro aiming

Settings → Controls → Gyro enables motion aiming on supported touch devices. Grant motion permission when requested. Choose Auto, Phone or Tablet tuning; adjust horizontal/vertical sensitivity, smoothing and invert options. Gyro adds to touch-drag looking and can be limited to aiming. If motion is unavailable, use touch drag; it remains fully usable.

### ⚙️ Reduce lag on slower devices

Choose Low graphics, then enable Auto quality if desired. For a lighter custom profile use render distance 3–4, pixel-ratio cap 1, resolution scale 0.5–0.75, low particles, and turn off clouds and shadows. Lower distance reduces streamed terrain; lower resolution reduces GPU work. These affect presentation rather than saved items or game rules. Frame rate depends on your device and project size.

### 📉 What Auto quality does

Auto watches sustained frame time during active play. If needed it gradually lowers phone/tablet pixel ratio, render distance, resolution, shadows, high particles and clouds, showing a message for changes. It does not raise quality again automatically. You can change settings manually at any time; large builds or busy combat may still need lighter settings.

### 🔧 Troubleshooting

For stutters, lower graphics, close heavy apps/tabs and keep the game foregrounded. For silent audio, tap the game and check browser and game volumes. For missing touch controls, check Settings → Controls → Touch. If the world is still loading, wait for nearby terrain before moving. If a room cannot connect, check the code, host connection and try another network. Save before refreshing.

## Saves & important rules

### 💾 Save and resume

Single-player worlds autosave about every 45 seconds. Build Home saves after edits and periodically. Pause, sleep and Save & Quit also save; Build Home supports Save now. Wait for the saved message before closing. On a save failure, Save & Quit keeps the session open so you can retry. Tab hiding attempts a save, but closing or losing power cannot guarantee it finishes.

### 🗄️ Where progress lives

Worlds, projects and blueprints are stored in this browser’s local site storage on this device; settings are local too. They do not sync through an account or between browsers/devices. Clearing site data removes them. If storage is blocked, the menu warns that the session uses temporary memory and progress will disappear when you close or reload.

### 🛟 Backups and connection

The game keeps one previous world-state backup for recovery if the current state is unreadable; this is not a full version history or an export feature. A loaded single-player session can run without internet, but reopening/refreshing may need the site to load again. Online rooms and voice need a connection. Deleting a world removes its local save.

### 🌊 World rules to remember

Water is generated scenery rather than a flowing-fluid simulation. Most wild creatures respawn naturally instead of being stored in a save; a tamed companion is saved. Crops use game time, not real-world offline time. Light and terrain visuals may briefly wait for chunk remeshing after edits. Regular saving and lighter graphics help keep long sessions comfortable.
